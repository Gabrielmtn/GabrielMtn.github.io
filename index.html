About Gabriel Martin

Hi, I'm Gabriel. The quote above is how I aspire to deliver value within a team.

I bring 15 years of experience across UX disciplines, engineering, design, and user research. This variety of experiences helps me bring diverse perspectives and expertise together, understand user and business needs, and have the technical and design chops to help teams transform complex challenges into elegant solutions that users love.

I believe the secret is to use that experience as a bridging force within cross-disciplinary teams, bringing everyone's vision together to deliver products that are technically robust, intuitive to use, and accessible to all.
