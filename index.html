      About Gabriel Martin

      Hi, I'm Gabriel, the quote above is how I aspire to deliver value within a team.

This variety of experiences helps me bring diverse perspectives and expertise together, understand user and business needs, and have the technical and design chops to help teams transform complex challenges into elegant solutions that users love.
